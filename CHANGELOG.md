# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `grpc.dart`: `GrpcChannelHolder`, `isLoopbackHost`, `ServiceException`,
  `guardGrpc`, `describeError`.
- `config.dart`: `BackendConfig` (the `backend_url` setting), `BuildInfo`.
- `intents.dart`: `IntentChannel` and parsing helpers for Android intents.
- `ui.dart`: `themesFromSeed`, `LoadingView`, `EmptyState`, `ErrorState`,
  `showMessage`, `confirmDialog`, `GitCommitFooter`, `GitCommitField`.
- `format.dart`: duration, stopwatch, byte, date and time formatting.
- `testing.dart`: `setUpInMemoryPrefs`, `wrapForTest`.
- Shared `analysis_options.yaml` and `taskfiles/flutter.yml`.
